About

From first lines of code to a software company.

I wrote my first web pages as a beginner developer — jQuery, PHP and a lot of trial and error. Years later, that curiosity grew into Infiforge Technologies, a Kenyan software and technology solutions company.

Today I lead a team building a family of products on one shared platform — a single auth system, one set of Flutter and Deno libraries, and twelve services from hospital management to agriculture to education.

I care about products that work: platform-native interfaces, reliable backends, and code that outlives the hype cycle.
